# SCRI Intellectual-Property, Publication and National Commercialisation Implementation Guide

> **Document role:** Shared implementation guide.
>
> **Source treatment:** This guide preserves the supplied strategic language verbatim where relevant. Formatting, headings, tables, links and implementation sequencing have been normalised for practical use.
>
> **Important:** This is a product and implementation guide, not legal advice. Kenyan intellectual-property, data-protection, financial-services and contracting decisions should be reviewed by suitably qualified Kenyan professionals.

## 1. Strategic implementation position

Yes. SCRI can be published as your intellectual work and commercialised as nationwide catastrophe-risk infrastructure. The strongest model has three layers:

1. A public reader edition that establishes your authorship and market thesis.
2. A proprietary intelligence platform containing the implementation advantage.
3. Licensed institutional partners that convert SCRI intelligence into insurance, investment and financing decisions.

Technically, SCRI contains a knowledge-based or expert-system layer, but it is broader than a classical expert system. It combines rules and institutional knowledge with computer vision, probabilistic state estimation, physical hazard models, actuarial modelling and operational workflows. Commercially, I would describe it as a catastrophe-risk intelligence utility or risk operating system—not merely SaaS.

The implementation objective is therefore to publish enough of the intellectual thesis to establish authorship, credibility and market leadership while preserving the code, calibrated models, data assets, operating methods and institutional integrations that create SCRI's commercial advantage.

## 2. Protecting the work while sharing it

Protect each component according to what it is:

| SCRI asset | Primary protection | Sharing approach |
|---|---|---|
| White paper, diagrams and original tables | Copyright | Publish a controlled reader edition |
| SCRI name, full brand and logo | Trademark | Display publicly after clearance |
| Source code and interface designs | Copyright, contracts and access controls | Demonstrations and controlled pilots |
| Model parameters, vulnerability curves and reliability algorithms | Trade secret | Confidential diligence or production access |
| Kenyan training data and derived datasets | Copyright/database rights, data licences and contracts | Aggregated outputs only |
| Novel technical inventions | Patent assessment before publication | Describe their function at a higher level |
| Partner exposure and claims data | Contract, confidentiality and data protection | Institution-specific secure workspaces |
| Community observations | Contribution terms and data governance | Consent-based permitted uses |

This asset-by-asset approach should be converted into an internal intellectual-property register. Each register entry should identify the asset, creator, creation date, current owner, protection mechanism, permitted disclosures, contractual dependencies, third-party components and the person responsible for maintaining its protection.

## 3. Recommended publication structure

Create three disclosure levels.

### Level 1 — Public reader edition

Publish:

- The catastrophe and livelihood problem.
- The SCRI product thesis.
- High-level system architecture.
- General equations and actuarial reasoning.
- Synthetic worked examples.
- Product-interface narratives.
- Governance and validation philosophy.
- Evidence sources and research conclusions.

This establishes Nevil Maloba as the author and thought leader without publishing the full implementation recipe.

The reader edition should carry consistent authorship, copyright, edition and citation information. Its diagrams and equations should explain what SCRI does and why it matters while omitting proprietary parameters, source-reliability thresholds, detailed feature engineering, customer-specific transformations and production configurations.

### Level 2 — Controlled diligence edition

Share under an NDA with insurers, reinsurers, investors and government partners:

- Model cards.
- Validation summaries.
- Technical schemas.
- Security architecture.
- Data availability assessments.
- Pilot economics.
- Selected model-performance evidence.
- Regulatory-perimeter analysis.

Every controlled disclosure should have a named recipient, purpose, version, disclosure date, authorised sender and contractual basis. Where practical, the diligence edition should use watermarks or recipient-specific identifiers and should avoid including information that the recipient does not need for the stated diligence purpose.

### Level 3 — Proprietary implementation

Keep within SCRI:

- Source code and deployment configuration.
- Trained model weights.
- Source-reliability algorithms.
- Deduplication and fraud rules.
- Detailed vulnerability functions.
- Claims and exposure transformations.
- Proprietary data mappings.
- Customer pricing and unit economics.
- Unpublished inventions and integration methods.

Access to this layer should follow role, customer, environment and purpose. Production secrets should remain outside public manuscripts and ordinary diligence packs. Internal documentation should still be complete enough to support continuity, security review, independent validation and investor diligence through controlled access.

## 4. Copyright and reader permission

Kenyan copyright protects eligible literary works, software, maps, plans and diagrams. Registration is voluntary, but the Copyright Register provides prima facie evidence of the registered particulars. The National Rights Registry accepts literary works and software documentation. See the [Kenya Copyright Act](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2001/12/eng%402022-12-31) and [National Rights Registry](https://nrr.copyright.go.ke/).

For your commercial objective, I recommend an all-rights-reserved reader licence rather than putting the entire project under an open licence:

> **Copyright © 2026 Nevil Maloba. All rights reserved.**
>
> Permission is granted to download, read, cite and share the complete, unmodified PDF for personal, educational and non-commercial research purposes, with full attribution. Commercial use, adaptation, extraction for product development, redistribution of modified versions, and use of proprietary models, datasets, diagrams or software require prior written permission from the author. Third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights and licences.

Have a Kenyan IP lawyer refine this notice. If you prefer a standard international licence, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 permits sharing with attribution while preventing commercial use and distribution of adaptations. It is irrevocable once granted, so the custom reader licence offers greater commercial control.

Also:

- Put “Nevil Maloba” in the PDF metadata, title page and copyright page.
- Assign every edition a version, publication date and cryptographic hash.
- Register the manuscript and important software releases with KECOBO's registry.
- Obtain an ISBN for the book edition and a DOI if you publish through a suitable repository.
- Keep dated Git history, source files, research notes and the claim ledger as evidence of development.

### Copyright implementation checklist

1. Freeze the publication candidate and assign its edition and version.
2. Verify author and rights-holder names across the title page, copyright page and file metadata.
3. Identify every third-party image, dataset, quotation, diagram and software component and record its licence or permission.
4. Add the final reader licence after legal review.
5. Generate and retain a cryptographic hash for the released PDF and its source bundle.
6. Register the relevant work with KECOBO's National Rights Registry.
7. Preserve the signed-off source, references, claim ledger and production record.
8. Record later corrections and releases as new, traceable editions.

## 5. Trademark and invention protection

Conduct a KIPI clearance search for:

- Spatial Catastrophe Risk Intelligence.
- SCRI.
- The logo.
- Names of individual product modules.

Then file the relevant word and device marks. Use ™ while claiming the brand; reserve ® for a registered mark. KIPI administers Kenyan patents, utility models and trademarks. See the [Kenya Industrial Property Institute](https://kipi.go.ke/) and its [trade-mark guidance](https://newsite.kipi.go.ke/trade-marks).

Patent assessment should happen before public release of any genuinely novel technical mechanism. Kenyan law requires novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability; prior public disclosure can become prior art. It also excludes mathematical methods, business methods and mere presentation of information from patentable inventions. A patent professional should therefore look for a concrete technical invention—such as a new sensor-fusion, edge-processing or spatial-computation mechanism—rather than trying to patent the broad SCRI business idea. See the [Industrial Property Act, sections 21–23](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2001/3/eng%402022-12-31).

### Pre-publication invention screen

Before releasing a new manuscript edition, technical paper, conference presentation, demonstration or public repository:

1. Identify technical mechanisms disclosed for the first time.
2. Separate broad concepts and mathematical reasoning from potentially patentable technical implementations.
3. Record the inventors, dates, problem solved, technical mechanism and available evidence of novelty.
4. Ask a patent professional whether any mechanism warrants prior filing or another form of protection.
5. Decide what can be disclosed publicly, what should be described at a higher level and what should remain confidential.
6. Record the decision and authorised disclosure scope in the intellectual-property register.

## 6. People, partner and company agreements

Every employee, contractor, researcher and implementation partner should sign documents covering:

- Confidentiality.
- Copyright and invention assignment.
- Background IP ownership.
- Newly created or “foreground” IP.
- Data ownership and permitted use.
- Publication approval.
- Rights after termination.
- Use of aggregated and derived data.

If you incorporate SCRI, investors will expect the company to own—or hold an exclusive, transferable commercial licence to—the core IP. You can remain publicly identified as author and founder while assigning the commercial rights to the company.

The contractual implementation should distinguish at least four things:

1. **Authorship and moral attribution:** Nevil Maloba remains publicly identified as the originator and author of the manuscript and product thesis, subject to the final legal structure.
2. **Commercial ownership or licence:** The operating company owns or holds sufficiently broad, exclusive and transferable rights to commercialise the platform.
3. **Background intellectual property:** Each contributor identifies tools, code, research or methods created before the engagement and the licence granted to SCRI.
4. **Foreground intellectual property:** Agreements state who owns new software, models, documentation, data transformations, interfaces and inventions produced during the engagement.

## 7. Implementation sequence

### Phase 1 — Establish ownership and evidence

- Create the SCRI intellectual-property and disclosure registers.
- Confirm authorship and ownership of the manuscript sources, diagrams, equations, code and design assets.
- Preserve dated source history, research notes and evidence ledgers.
- Identify third-party content and dependencies.
- Decide whether the commercial rights will remain personally owned, be assigned to a company or be licensed exclusively to it.

**Output:** A documented chain of title for the public work and the proprietary product.

### Phase 2 — Define the publication boundary

- Classify material into the public reader, controlled diligence and proprietary implementation levels.
- Review equations, diagrams, appendices and worked examples for proprietary detail.
- Replace sensitive real data with synthetic or properly authorised evidence in the reader edition.
- Add edition, version, date, author, copyright and permission language.
- Complete the invention screen before release.

**Output:** A publication-ready reader edition with an explicit disclosure boundary.

### Phase 3 — Register and protect

- Register eligible manuscript and software materials with the National Rights Registry.
- Conduct KIPI searches for SCRI names, marks and module brands.
- File appropriate trademark applications after professional clearance.
- Obtain professional patent advice for any qualifying technical invention before disclosure.
- Consider ISBN and DOI arrangements for the citable book and research editions.

**Output:** A documented portfolio of copyright records, brand applications and invention decisions.

### Phase 4 — Establish controlled diligence

- Prepare the NDA and disclosure procedure.
- Build the diligence edition from approved technical, validation, security, economic and regulatory materials.
- Apply recipient, purpose, version and access controls.
- Maintain a disclosure log and withdrawal or expiry process.

**Output:** A repeatable institutional diligence pathway that protects proprietary implementation details.

### Phase 5 — Secure the implementation network

- Put employment, contractor, research and partner agreements in place before work begins.
- Define background and foreground IP, data rights, publication controls and post-termination duties.
- Separate partner data into institution-specific workspaces.
- Establish contributor terms and consent-based permitted uses for community observations.
- Protect production code, models, weights, parameters and customer economics through technical and contractual controls.

**Output:** An implementation environment in which contributors, partners, data and commercial rights have clear ownership and permitted uses.

### Phase 6 — Prepare national commercialisation

- Position SCRI as a catastrophe-risk intelligence utility or risk operating system.
- Publish the reader edition to establish the intellectual and market thesis.
- Use the controlled diligence edition to qualify institutional partners and investors.
- Commercialise proprietary capabilities through the SCRI company and appropriately licensed institutional partners.
- Keep regulated insurance, investment and financing decisions with institutions holding the relevant authority.

**Output:** A three-layer national commercial model connecting public intellectual leadership, proprietary implementation and licensed institutional execution.

- Nevil Maloba's authorship is consistently recorded in the manuscript, metadata and publication record.
