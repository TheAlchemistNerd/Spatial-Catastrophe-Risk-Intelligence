# Draft inquiry to the KBA Research Centre

**To:** research@kba.co.ke  
**Subject:** Inquiry: Spatial catastrophe intelligence and bank credit-risk research paper

Dear KBA Research Team,

I am writing to seek your guidance on the most appropriate route for a research paper provisionally titled **“Can Spatial Catastrophe Intelligence Improve Bank Credit-Risk Identification in Kenya? A Flood-and-Drought Empirical Framework.”**

The paper develops a Kenya-specific method for linking point-in-time flood and drought evidence to conventional banking-risk outcomes. It combines authoritative environmental observations, earth observation, governed community intelligence and probabilistic hazard estimates with borrower and collateral exposure. The empirical design tests whether these spatial features improve the out-of-sample identification and calibration of arrears, restructuring, stage migration, default or recovery relative to conventional sectoral, county and macro-climate indicators. It also examines how insurance and verified resilience measures may moderate loss and recovery.

The work aligns most directly with the 15th Annual Banking Research Conference sub-theme on **Risk Identification and Management**, including climate-related risks, banking resilience and pathways towards stability. Its transparent baseline-and-challenger design may also be suitable for the **Methodological Session**. A mature six-part SCRI research architecture and actuarial framework already support the proposed paper; the bank-focused empirical study is now being developed.

I recognise that the formal 2026 proposal and full-paper milestones have passed. I would be grateful for your advice on whether the work could be considered through one of the following routes:

1. a late contribution to the 2026 Methodological Session, if space and review arrangements permit;
2. submission to the KBA Working Paper Series after completion of the empirical study; or
3. preparation for the next Annual Banking Research Conference cycle.

I have attached a one-page concept note and can share the five-page research proposal, full SCRI manuscript, proposed data dictionary and methodology on request. I would also welcome a short discussion with the Centre on research fit and potential data or industry-engagement pathways.
