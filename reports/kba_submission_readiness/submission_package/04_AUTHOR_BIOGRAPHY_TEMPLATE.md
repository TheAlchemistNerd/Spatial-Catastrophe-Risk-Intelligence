# Author Biography for KBA Submission

## Nevil Maloba

Nevil Maloba is the Founder of Philtechent LTD, the company developing Spatial Catastrophe Risk Intelligence (SCRI). He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Actuarial Science from the University of Eldoret.

His current research focuses on the relationship between catastrophe observation, artificial intelligence, probabilistic hazard modelling, actuarial modelling and financial resilience. Through SCRI, he is developing a Kenya-first research and product architecture that connects environmental and community observations with flood, drought and other catastrophe states; exposure and vulnerability; insurance and credit risk; and sustainable and resilience finance.

Nevil is also the author of *Mwendo Pamoja: Continuous Underwriting*, a narrative and technical white paper exploring continuous risk intelligence across insurance, credit and institutional decision systems. His SCRI work extends this interdisciplinary approach into multi-hazard catastrophe intelligence, with particular attention to Kenyan livelihoods, financial institutions, communities and investable adaptation.

The proposed KBA research examines whether spatially and temporally resolved flood and drought intelligence can improve the identification and calibration of climate-related credit risk in Kenyan bank portfolios. It links physical hazard evidence to borrower cash flow, collateral, repayment, recovery, PD, LGD, EAD, expected credit loss and resilience-finance applications.
